# Go-to-market

Lucy's lane (docs/TEAM.md § Go-to-market). What belongs here: anything
about reaching people and agents that should be versioned beside the code
and reviewed like it, such as launch checklists, directory and registry
listings as submitted, messaging that the site's pages quote, and the briefs
of the agents that do outreach.

What does not belong here: contacts, drafts and approvals (they live in the
outreach console's own store, never in a public repository), and anything
about plans that are not public yet.

Standing rules, from `CLAUDE.md`: every promotional post and every email is
a draft that needs per-item approval by the owner or by Lucy; recipient
addresses come from the work itself, never from scraping; and nothing here
may claim more than the record shows.
